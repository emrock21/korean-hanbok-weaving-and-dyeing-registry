# Korean Hanbok Weaving & Dyeing Traditions Registry

This repository preserves, in an immutable and decentralized format, the weaving and dyeing traditions that form the textile foundation of the Korean hanbok. These techniques — Hansan mosi, oksa silk, nubi quilting, jogakbo patchwork, Jeju meok dyeing, jjok indigo, and others — represent centuries of regional craftsmanship, ecological adaptation, and symbolic color philosophy.

Many of these traditions were endangered during the 20th century due to industrialization, synthetic dyes, and the decline of handloom weaving. Documenting them on-chain ensures that their technical, cultural, and regional identities remain accessible for future generations.

## Why Hanbok Textile Traditions Matter

Hanbok weaving and dyeing are:

- a **regional identity system**, with Hansan, Andong, Jeonju, Jeju, and Naju as major centers  
- a **material philosophy**, balancing ramie, silk, cotton, and natural dyes  
- a **symbolic color grammar**, rooted in the five-directional palette *obangsaek*  
- a **technical lineage**, transmitted through guilds and hereditary artisans  
- a **survivor of industrial decline**, preserved through community workshops and master artisans  

Hansan Mosi is recognized as **UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage**.

## Purpose of This Contract

The goal of this registry is to:

1. **Create a permanent, censorship-resistant record** of Korean textile traditions.  
2. **Preserve endangered weaving and dyeing knowledge** with technical precision.  
3. **Document materials, techniques, motifs, and cultural context** in structured form.  
4. **Provide an immutable reference** for museums, researchers, artisans, and future generations.  
5. **Honor the regional diversity of Korean textile craftsmanship.**

The contract is intentionally:

- **ownerless** — no authority can alter or censor the registry  
- **payment-free** — no monetization, no barriers  
- **minimalistic and secure** — no external calls  
- **permanent** — entries become part of the cultural ledger  

## What Each Entry Records

- **textileName** — mosi, oksa, nubi, jogakbo, meok dyeing, jjok indigo  
- **region** — Hansan, Andong, Jeonju, Jeju, Naju  
- **materials** — ramie, silk, cotton, natural dyes  
- **techniques** — handloom weaving, quilting, patchwork, fermentation dyeing  
- **motifs** — geometric, obangsaek color logic, natural textures  
- **culturalContext** — ceremonial hanbok, seasonal garments, ritual cloths  
- **uniqueness** — UNESCO status, regional identity, rare techniques
